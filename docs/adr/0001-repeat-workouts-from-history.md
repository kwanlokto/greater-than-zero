# Workouts are repeated from History; Templates and Targets are removed

A lifter starts a Workout by repeating a finished one, or from empty, and Workouts of one kind share a Category the lifter names. Templates, their Targets and the "update template?" and "raise the target?" offers are removed. After two weeks of use, the only way to do a session again was to save it as one more Template, which bred near-identical Templates and a Start section that showed names with nothing behind them. One idea, History, proved easier to hold than two.

## Considered Options

- **Keep Templates and add "repeat a finished Workout" beside them.** Rejected: two ways to do the same thing, and the similar Templates would still need grouping.
- **Group Workouts by their Exercises instead of a named Category.** Rejected as the main grouping: one swapped Exercise would split a group, and a Rotation needs names. It's kept only for Workouts with no Category.

## Consequences

- A plan can't be written ahead for a Workout never done. A new kind of Workout is done once from empty, then repeated.
- Planned Sets come from the Workout repeated, so a worse session lowers the next one's starting numbers. The old rule that a Target never drops is gone. The "ready to go heavier" hint survives on a rep range kept on each Exercise.
- Converting is one-way. Template Exercise lists and Targets are deleted on update and when an older Backup file is imported. Template names live on as Categories, with their Workouts and their places in Rotations.
