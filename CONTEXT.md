# Greater Than Zero

A lifter's own training and food log, kept on their phone: Workouts planned from Templates, Meals counted against Macro targets, and daily Weigh-ins.

## Language

**Template workout**:
A Workout started from a Template. It's an editable copy: changes made on the day reach the Template only if the lifter accepts an offer when finishing it.
_Avoid_: Template session

**Exercise entry**:
One Exercise as done in one Workout, holding the Sets logged for it. "Entry" on its own always means this.
